# Labour Domain — Sources

## Important: the primary law changed on 21 November 2025

The four new Labour Codes are now in force nationwide, and the 29 old
central laws they replace — including the Payment of Wages Act 1936,
Minimum Wages Act 1948, Industrial Disputes Act 1947, and Factories Act
1948 — stand repealed. Download the **Codes**, not the old Acts, as your
primary source.

The catch: central and most state *rules* (the detailed implementing
regulations) are still being finalized — only a handful of states have
notified final rules, most are still in draft. During this gap, the old
Acts' provisions continue to apply wherever a matter isn't yet covered by
finalized rules. This is genuinely a live, uneven transition, not a clean
swap — which makes it a good real test of your Response Agent's
"jurisdiction/timeliness may vary" caveat.

## judgments/

https://indiankanoon.org — search terms below. 5–7 short summarized
excerpts, same approach as Consumer. Real starting points, verified:

- **Bangalore Water Supply & Sewerage Board v. A. Rajappa** (1978) 2 SCC
  213 — defined the "Triple Test" for what counts as an "industry" under
  the Industrial Disputes Act
- **Surendra Kumar Verma v. Central Government Industrial Tribunal-cum-
  Labour Court** (1980) 4 SCC 443 — reinstatement should ordinarily come
  with back wages when termination is set aside
- **Workmen of Meenakshi Mills v. Meenakshi Mills** (1994) 2 SCC 422 —
  upheld "last come, first go" retrenchment rules as reasonable
- **U.P. State Brassware Corpn. Ltd. v. Udai Narain Pandey** (8 December
  2005) — reinstatement with back wages is not automatic; courts weigh
  the facts
- **People's Union for Democratic Rights v. Union of India** (1982) —
  the "Asiad Workers" case; paying less than minimum wage can amount to
  forced labour under Article 23
- **M.C. Mehta v. State of Tamil Nadu** (1996) 6 SCC 756 — landmark
  child labour judgment, established a welfare/rehabilitation fund model

Search terms if you want more: "wrongful termination Supreme Court
India", "retrenchment compensation labour court", "unpaid wages labour
court judgment".
