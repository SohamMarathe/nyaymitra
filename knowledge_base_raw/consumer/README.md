# Consumer Domain — Sources

Start here. Consumer law has the clearest, most linear procedures, which
makes it the easiest domain to validate your whole pipeline against before
adding more.

## acts/

| File to save as | Get it from |
|---|---|
| `consumer_protection_act_2019.pdf` | https://www.indiacode.nic.in — search "Consumer Protection Act 2019" and download the PDF |
| `consumer_protection_rules_2020.pdf` | Department of Consumer Affairs: https://consumeraffairs.nic.in (search "Consumer Protection Rules 2020") |
| `consumer_protection_ecommerce_rules_2020.pdf` | Same portal — the E-Commerce Rules are a common source of citizen questions |

## procedures/

Write these yourself as clean `.txt` files, sourced from:
- https://consumerhelpline.gov.in — National Consumer Helpline (NCH). Look
  for their citizen guidance pages on how to file a complaint at the
  District/State/National Commission level.
- https://ncdrc.nic.in — National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.
  Their site has procedural notices and forms.

Suggested file: `district_forum_filing_procedure.txt` — summarize the
step-by-step process (where to file, what documents, fee structure by claim
value, timelines) in your own words.

## judgments/

Go to https://indiankanoon.org and search terms like "consumer protection
deficiency of service" or "unfair trade practice". Court judgments are
public record; a short, honestly-summarized excerpt for a non-commercial
student project is standard practice, but avoid copy-pasting large
verbatim blocks.

Real starting points, verified — 7 is a good target count:

- **Indian Medical Association v. V.P. Shantha** (1995) 6 SCC 651 —
  medical services fall within "service" under the Consumer Protection Act
- **Karnataka Power Transmission Corp. v. Ashok Iron Works Pvt. Ltd.**
  (9 Feb 2009), (2009) 3 SCC 240 — widened "consumer" to include companies
  buying for commercial use, not just individuals
- **Ghaziabad Development Authority v. Balbir Singh** (2004) 4 SCC 65 —
  set standards for compensation in deficiency-of-service cases against
  development authorities
- **M/S Imperia Structures Ltd. v. Anil Patni** (2 Nov 2020) — Consumer
  Protection Act remedies are additional to, not replaced by, RERA remedies
- **Ireo Grace Realtech Pvt. Ltd. v. Abhishek Khanna** (11 Jan 2021) —
  further clarified the Consumer Protection Act / RERA relationship
- **Samruddhi Co-operative Housing Society Ltd. v. Mumbai Mahalaxmi
  Construction Pvt. Ltd.** (2022 SCC OnLine SC 35, decided 11 Jan 2022) —
  failure to provide an occupancy certificate is a deficiency in service
- **Bar of Indian Lawyers v. D.K. Gandhi PS National Institute of
  Communicable Diseases** (14 May 2024) — advocates are NOT liable under
  the Consumer Protection Act for service deficiency (useful edge-case:
  shows the Act's limits, not just its reach)

## faqs/

Write these yourself — they're the easiest to produce and often the
highest-value content for retrieval, since they're phrased the way real
users ask questions. Suggested starting set (5–10 short Q&A entries in one
`.txt` file, `common_questions.txt`):
- What can I do if I received a defective product?
- How long do I have to file a consumer complaint?
- Can I file a consumer complaint for a service, not just a product?
- What's the difference between District, State, and National Commission?
- Do I need a lawyer to file a consumer complaint?
